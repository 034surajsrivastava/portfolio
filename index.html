01 — The person behind the code

Most of my work is invisible. That is the part I care about.

SS

I am a second-year B.Tech student in Computer Science and Engineering, specialising in Data Science and AI, at a university in Lucknow.

I am drawn to the question of what happens after a button is clicked: the data that moves, the structure that holds it, and the reasoning that decides what comes next. Interfaces are the surface. The engineering underneath is where I spend most of my attention.

I prefer software and AI over hardware-heavy projects, because I like building things where the logic can be tested, measured and improved.
